## Interview framing

For each project, explain the user problem, the system boundary, the AI decision, the evaluation contract, one failure mode, the deployment path, and the next limitation. Avoid leading with a technology list. Lead with the engineering trade-off and the evidence.

## Do not overclaim

Do not describe small-user validation as production scale, synthetic negative examples as ground truth, or AI-generated scores as validated business metrics. State the current evidence and the next measurement explicitly.
